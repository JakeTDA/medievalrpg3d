- Dungeon crawler game where you (and maybe friends) go into dungeons or areas like dungeons and get loot or doing something for an objective
- You use the loot you get along the way to go deeper
- Punishing game that doesn't have traditional normal health bars but maybe with body parts, limbs, and injuries

- Classes:
- Melee
	- Knight - The tank and the main fighter of the classes, either can use a two-handed long sword or a one-handed sword and shield. It will have moves that attract enemies to them, as well as generally being tank-y because of their plate armor. They may also have Blessed Magic, basically the power of the lord, that can be useful against certain enemies, and generally be like a paladin. TLDR; badass cleaving thru enemies
	- Rogue - The fast and stealthy scout, has a dagger that can be used for combat but would mainly be used for assassinations (It maybe does more dmg if backstabbed or in stealth). It also has many useful tools like lockpicks, trap checking abilities, enemy detection abilities from enhanced senses, etc. It will also have throwing knives, which does minor damage to enemies, but can be useful in a pinch, and maybe some daggers have special effects such as poison, fire, etc. that will change in effectiveness based on the enemy type.  Also be able to disarm and put down traps. TLDR; stealthy thief with trickery tricks
- Ranged
	- Ranger - The bow user that can pick enemies off from afar. It will be able to kill a lot of enemies with headshots, but the bow won't be as easy as shooting a gun and it will have mechanics that have sway and hard to aim stuff. It will also have limited arrows which will make the ranger be more resourceful, and will also have a dagger as a backup weapon. Archer can also be stealthy but doesn't have trick style things like Rogue, but instead it works as a long range recon that can snipe enemies, and be a good way to start a battle. It will also have nature style magic such as wood magic, beast magic. TLDR; natural bow guy, green arrow but fantasy
	- Mage - The all powerful Magic user, able to wield elemental magics and more. The mage would have a magic staff, which can cast different types of magic. But, there will be a specific casting system that would allow manipulation of magic, and the magic staff will have slots inside which has the "magic pellet" which will be the source of the magic like elements, and some slots above that which are manipulators, and all of it goes to the top of the staff which will create the magic based on the element and manipulation. So you have to pre think of what you may need and manipulate the magic based on that. This WILL be complex.
